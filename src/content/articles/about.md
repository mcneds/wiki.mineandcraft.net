**mineandcraft.net** contains guides for playing on the server and a directory of its players.

## Reading and contributing

The wiki is public to read. Its pages are maintained by the server owner. If you spot an error or have a story, build, or screenshot to add, pass it to the owner through your existing server contact.

## How information is checked

The initial guides use the owner's server information, supplied mod and datapack inventory, official project documentation, and the uploaded crafting-tweaks archive. Where a setting has not been checked on the running server, the relevant article says so.
