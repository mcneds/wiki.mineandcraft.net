## Be civil.

That's the rule.

mineandcraft.net is a shared survival world with a Hermitcraft-like community spirit. Treat the people you play with, their time, and their creations with consideration.

If you're unsure whether something is welcome, talk to the people involved first. If something goes wrong, speak to the server owner.

## Joining

Everyone needs an invitation, whether playing Java or Bedrock. See [Getting started](/wiki/getting-started/) for connection information.

*This page reflects the rule and invitation policy provided by the server owner.*
