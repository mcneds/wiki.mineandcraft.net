These gameplay mods add a few useful conveniences while keeping the familiar survival loop.

## Infinite Trading

Trade with villagers through their normal trading screen. **Infinite Trading** prevents their offers from locking after repeated use when its villager option is enabled.

The mod also supports wandering traders, enabled by default upstream. Server configuration can change either setting. The mod does not mean trades are free or that prices never change.

## Better Than Mending

Hold an item enchanted with **Mending**, then **sneak and right-click** to repair it using experience you already have.

You need the enchantment and available experience. This guide does not promise that unenchanted equipment can be repaired this way. The exact Bedrock control interaction has not been tested on this server.

## NetherPortalFix

Travel through a Nether portal, then return through the same arrival portal. **NetherPortalFix** remembers the portal you originally entered and directs you back to it.

- It applies to players, not mobs, items, or vehicles.
- It does not change the normal 1:8 Overworld-to-Nether coordinate ratio.
- If the original return portal is gone or no longer valid, normal portal lookup is used.

## Unlock all recipes

The **Unlock All Recipes** datapack unlocks the recipe book's recipes when you start playing. You still need the ingredients to craft items. For changed recipes, see [Crafting tweaks](/wiki/crafting-tweaks/).
