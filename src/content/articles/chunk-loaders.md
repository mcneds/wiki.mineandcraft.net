The **Chunk Loaders** datapack adds a “loadstone”: a lodestone that keeps its own chunk loaded until it is broken.

## Make a loadstone

1. Place a **lodestone** in the chunk you want to keep loaded.
2. **Drop a nether star above the lodestone** to turn it into a chunk loader.

This is the Vanilla Tweaks datapack mechanic, not a recipe or interface from a similarly named mod.

## Remove a loadstone

Break the loadstone to stop it from keeping its chunk loaded. Item refunds and other removal details have not been verified, so this guide does not promise a nether-star refund.

## What to expect

The author's description guarantees that the loadstone's chunk stays loaded. It does not promise that every farm works without a nearby player. Player-dependent spawning and random-tick behavior should be checked for your particular farm rather than assumed.

Check with the owner before leaving many loaders active; no numerical server limit has been supplied.
